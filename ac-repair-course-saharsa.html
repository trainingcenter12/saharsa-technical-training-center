About this course

Learn AC repair the practical way

Air conditioners are in demand in every town. This module teaches you how a split AC works and how to diagnose and fix real faults, not just theory.

Classroom theory is followed by workshop sessions on real AC units. Along with AC, the same 2-month course also covers refrigerator, washing machine and geyser repair.
