About this course

Learn AC repair the practical way

Air conditioners are in demand in every town. This module teaches you how a split AC works and how to diagnose and fix real faults, not just theory.

Classroom theory is followed by workshop sessions on real AC units. Along with AC, the same 2-month course also covers refrigerator, washing machine and geyser repair.

See the full course

What you will learn

AC repair syllabus

Skills covered

  • Identifying split AC indoor and outdoor unit parts
  • Electrical testing of AC components
  • Basic fault diagnosis
  • Installation fundamentals
  • Servicing procedures

Practical topics

  • Refrigerant-system concepts
  • Installation practice
  • Gas charging demonstration
  • Cleaning and routine servicing

Tools you will use

  • Multimeter and clamp meter
  • Vacuum pump
  • Gauge set
  • Pipe cutting and flaring tools

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where can I learn AC repair in Saharsa?

Saharsa Technical Training Centre is at Kahara Kutti, Bangaon Road, Saharsa 852201, Bihar. We teach AC repair in a practical 2-month course.

What is the AC repair course fee?

Rs 10,000 without stay. With stay (room, bedsheet and utensils) it is Rs 12,000, or Rs 10,000 if you pay in one go.

Is there a free demo class?

Yes. Attend the class for 3 days free, with no payment. Take admission only if you like the training.

Do I learn other appliances too?

Yes. The same 2-month course also covers refrigerator, washing machine and geyser repair.

Can I pay in two instalments?

Yes. With stay you can pay Rs 6,000 and then Rs 6,000, total Rs 12,000. The Rs 2,000 discount applies only on one-time payment.

Do I need prior experience?

No. Anyone can join.

Is food provided?

Utensils are provided and students cook their own food. Food is not served by the institute.
