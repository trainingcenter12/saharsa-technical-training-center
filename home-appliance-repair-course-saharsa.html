How the two months are organised

From basics to real service work

1

Basics and safety

Electrical safety, how current flows, using a multimeter and reading simple circuit diagrams before touching any appliance.

2

Know the parts

Identify motors, capacitors, thermostats, relays, heating elements and control boards, and learn what each one does.

3

Test and diagnose

Test parts one by one, understand common symptoms and learn a step-by-step method to reach the actual fault.

4

Repair and service

Replace parts, service units and refit them, practising on real appliances in the workshop with trainer guidance.

5

Customer-ready skills

Learn to explain a fault clearly, give a fair estimate and finish a job neatly so customers trust you.

Why learn all four together

Why one course for four appliances makes sense

Most appliance faults come from a small set of parts: motors, capacitors, thermostats, relays, heating elements, switches and control boards. Once you can test these safely, you can work on many kinds of appliances. That is why we teach AC, refrigerator, washing machine and geyser together instead of in separate short courses.

It also helps with real work. A household that calls you for a fridge today may need an AC service next summer and a geyser repair next winter. A technician who can handle all four earns the trust of the same family for years.

If you want to focus on one appliance first, you can read the page for AC repair, refrigerator repair, washing machine repair or geyser repair. The training itself stays one combined course.

Frequently asked questions

What is the home appliance repair course in Saharsa?

It is a 2-month practical course at Saharsa Technical Training Centre that teaches repair of air conditioners, refrigerators, washing machines and geysers in one combined programme.

The training is one combined course covering all four appliances. You can read each appliance page to see its syllabus, and attend the free demo to see how classes run.

What time are the classes?
